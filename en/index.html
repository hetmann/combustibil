FAQ

Good to know.

Need a hand? ↗
Which receipts are supported?

The app connects to OMV and Petrom invoicing services in Romania. Receipt eligibility and invoicing deadlines are determined by the provider.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, to verify receipts, look up companies, submit requests and download documents. Your history and downloaded files are available offline.

Is the downloaded document the final invoice?

A portal document may be an invoice request. Check the invoice status with the provider and, where applicable, in SPV.

Do I need an account?

No. Company details, history and documents are stored locally. The app contacts invoicing providers and Expo for technical updates.
