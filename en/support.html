How do I use the app?

In Bon, scan a receipt QR code or enter receipt details manually and verify it. Select a company and check the information before confirming an invoice request. Firme manages company billing details. Istoric shows local records and downloaded documents. No app account is required.

Receipt not found or rejected

Check the number, amount, provider and internet connection. Messages about invoicing periods and receipt validity reflect the invoicing service’s rules. For receipt eligibility and invoice corrections, contact the issuer through the OMV portal or Petrom portal.

A request has an unknown result

A connection failure does not prove issuance failed. Do not resubmit until the provider clarifies the outcome, to avoid duplicates. The app does not automatically retry issuance after a timeout.

Is the downloaded PDF the final invoice?

A portal document may be an invoice request. Check the document and invoice status with the provider and, where applicable, in SPV. The app does not automatically confirm registration of a final invoice in SPV.

Camera and offline access

Enable camera permission in device settings or enter the receipt manually. Local history and documents already downloaded remain available offline. Verification and issuance require internet access.

Deletion and privacy

Read the deletion instructions, privacy policy and terms and conditions. Local deletion does not cancel invoices or submitted requests.
