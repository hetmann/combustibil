Privacy Policy

Updated on 25 September 2026.

Who we are and how to contact us

Combustibil is provided by HETMANN TECHNOLOGIES SRL, Romanian tax identification number 38163803, registered office: Calea Turzii 162-168, Bl. C1, Ap. 93, Cluj-Napoca, Cluj, Romania. Send privacy requests to this address, marked “Privacy — Combustibil”. Company website: hetmann.tech.

We are responsible for processing we determine to provide the app. Combustibil is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by OMV or Petrom. The invoicing provider processes information it receives for its own purposes, including tax obligations.

Information used by the app

No account is required. The app stores on your device company billing details entered or retrieved using a tax identification number (CUI), such as name, tax number and address; receipt number, date, amount and responses from the invoicing service; requests and their status; document identifiers and access tokens; optional receipt photos and downloaded documents.

Company information may include personal data concerning individuals, representatives or sole traders. Only provide information needed for invoicing. The app does not request a full payment card number or card security code.

When information is transmitted

Receipt verification sends the receipt number and amount to OMV/Petrom invoicing services. Company lookup sends the CUI. Confirming an invoice request sends company details and receipt information returned by the provider. Document retrieval uses the document identifier and access token. Connections use HTTPS; receiving servers also receive technical connection information such as your IP address.

Requests go directly from your device to the invoicing services, without an app-operated intermediary server. Provider identification may first query OMV and then Petrom when the first service reports a brand mismatch. The app does not upload receipt photos to these services.

The services are operated by OMV Petrom Marketing SRL. See the provider’s notices and services: OMV privacy, OMV invoicing portal, Petrom invoicing portal. A portal document may be an invoice request; its availability does not establish that a final invoice exists in the Romanian tax authority’s SPV system.

Updates and external services

The app uses Expo EAS Update to check for and download app code and resources. These requests contain technical metadata, such as platform, app version/runtime, project ID, technical installation or update identifiers and IP address. The app does not attach receipts, company details, photos or PDF documents to update requests.

Expo may process data outside the European Economic Area under its privacy policy, which describes its transfer and retention arrangements. See also Expo privacy explained.

The app has no advertising, advertising tracking, usage analytics or automatic error reporting through Expo Observe. Operating systems and app stores may provide diagnostics governed by their own settings and policies. We do not sell personal data.

When you choose to open or share a document, the selected application and recipient receive it. Their subsequent processing is governed by their own practices. External websites apply their own policies.

Purposes and legal grounds

Information is used to provide requested functions: receipt organisation, billing details, invoice requests and document access. Where personal data is involved, the legal basis is performance of the service requested by the individual (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in providing the service to users representing a company (Article 6(1)(f)). Technical updates and duplicate-request prevention serve the legitimate interest of keeping the app functional and secure.

We do not use data for profiling or automated decisions producing legal effects concerning individuals. Receipt validation supports the invoicing service. The provider determines its own legal grounds for tax processing.

Camera, storage and security

Camera access is requested when scanning or photographing a receipt. You can refuse or revoke permission in device settings and enter receipt details manually. Photos are optional. The app does not require location or microphone access.

Data and files are stored in the app’s local storage, protected by the operating system. The app does not add its own database encryption. Protect your device and any exported documents. There is no app account or cloud history synchronisation. Device backups depend on the platform and its settings; Android app backup is disabled in the current configuration.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app’s storage. Deleting a receipt removes its record and associated local files. For a submitted transaction, the app retains the transaction number locally to prevent duplicates. Deleting a company does not remove its details from previous requests.

Completely removing app storage, including through uninstalling where supported by the platform, also removes these local records. Backups may restore data. Exported or shared copies must be deleted separately. We cannot access or remotely delete history stored exclusively on your phone. See data deletion instructions.

Local deletion does not withdraw requests already sent or delete the provider’s fiscal records. Providers apply their own legal retention duties. Expo applies the retention terms in its privacy policy to technical data it processes.

Support correspondence

If you contact us, we use your contact information and message to respond and handle the request. This is based on our legitimate interest in providing support and, for statutory requests, compliance with legal obligations. Correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable record-keeping or legal-claim requirements, then deleted or anonymised. Do not send document access tokens, card details or unnecessary receipt copies.

Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Contact us at the address above. We normally respond within one month and will communicate any legally permitted extension. Requests concerning providers’ independent processing can also be addressed directly to them. You may complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP, or another competent supervisory authority.

Children and changes

The app is intended for users managing business billing and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly seek children’s personal data. Contact us if you believe such data has been provided to us.

We update this policy when functions or data practices change and show the revision date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its information page.

Hosting of these pages

These information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address and request details, to deliver and secure its service. The pages contain no analytics, advertising scripts or cookies implemented by us. Infrastructure data processing, retention and any international transfers are described in the GitHub privacy statement.
