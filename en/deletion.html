Data deletion

There is no account to delete

Combustibil does not offer account creation or store history on its own server. You manage data on your device. HETMANN TECHNOLOGIES SRL cannot remotely erase information stored exclusively on your phone.

Delete a receipt or company

In Istoric, open the receipt, choose local deletion and confirm. Its record and associated local photos/PDFs are removed. For submitted transactions, the transaction number remains locally to prevent duplicates.

In Firme, use the company’s red delete button and confirm. Deleting a company does not change its details in previous requests.

Remove all local data

On Android, open system settings, select Combustibil, open storage settings and clear app data, or uninstall the app. Labels vary by device.

On iOS, use Delete App. Offload App retains documents and data and is not equivalent to erasing them. Export important documents before deleting the app.

Completely clearing storage also removes local duplicate-prevention records. Backups may restore data; check platform settings separately. Exported, shared or externally saved copies must be deleted from the relevant apps or destinations.

Information already sent to providers

Local deletion does not cancel a request or delete fiscal documents held by OMV/Petrom. Contact the provider for information it holds; tax obligations may require retention. Expo update metadata is governed by Expo’s privacy policy.

Personal data requests
